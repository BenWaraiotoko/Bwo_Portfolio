## The short version

So here's the thing — this website is a personal portfolio. It doesn't want your data, doesn't need it, and honestly wouldn't know what to do with it.

- **No analytics.** No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, nothing phoning home about your visit.
- **No cookies set by this site.** If you see cookies, they came from the CDN in front of it (Cloudflare), not from me.
- **No accounts, no forms, no newsletter.** There's nothing to sign up for, so there's nothing to leak.
- **No third-party data sharing.** Your visit isn't sold, shared, or "anonymized and aggregated for insights." It's just... a visit.

## The Google Calendar OAuth app (hermes-calendar)

This site is listed as the home page of a small Google Cloud OAuth application (`hermes-calendar-XXXXXX`) that connects a personal automation agent to my own calendar. Since that app is technically "in production," Google requires a privacy policy link — so here it is, for an app whose entire user base is one person: me.

What that app can access: **my calendar, and nothing else.** Scope is `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar`, granted by me, to me. No Gmail, no Drive, no contacts. If you are reading this and you are not me — congratulations on finding a page almost nobody will ever visit, but rest assured the app can't see any of your data, because Google only lets it touch the calendar of the account that clicked "Allow." That account is mine.

## What this site stores

The site itself is static (Quartz). It's served from your browser's cache and a CDN. There is no database, no server-side session, nothing server-side at all — just files.
